Ideal Merc Options - by Captainlance9
Source: https://mobalytics.gg/poe/builds/captainlance9-luminary-merc-bot

    This covers mercs I have personally tested but many more can be quite strong, I will be expanding this list prior to the next league launch to give way more options.
    Kineticist
        BiS Clear Merc
        Kinetic Blast of Clustering
         - Multiple Projectiles Support
         - Returning Projectiles Support
         - Clear Support(pierce/chain/ect)
        do NOT get Kinetic Bolt
         - this will brick merc ai to not use clustering properly
        Buff Skills: Haste
         / Inspiring Cry
    Manyshot
        Good Clear / Single Target
        Ice Shot
         - Multiple Projectiles Support
         - Returning Projectiles Support
         - Elemental Damage with Attacks Support
        Vaal Ice Shot
        (single target needed) - Returning Projectiles Support
         - Elemental Damage with Attacks Support
         - Cooldown Recovery Support
        AVOID "icicle rain" this skill does bad damage and interrupts vaal ice shot from occurring properly
        Buff Skills: Grace
         / Hatred
         / Frost Bomb
    Blade Ambusher
        Good Bossing / Single Target Merc
        Spectral Helix of Trarthus
         - Multiple Traps Support
         - Trap and Mine Damage Support
         - Slower Projectiles Support
        Buff Skills: Grace
         / Summon Skitterbots
    Combatant
        Good All rounder / Starter Merc (better clear option as armour stack setup late game)
        Static Strike
         - Elemental Damage with Attacks Support
         - More Duration Support
         - Chain Support
        Frost Blades
         - Returning Projectiles Support
         - Elemental Damage with Attacks Support
         - Chain Support
        /
        Buff Skills: Wrath
         / Purity of Ice
